# Taxis

## What to look for
- Yellow, green, or white sedans/sedans with taxi markings
- "TAXI" text on roof sign or door panels
- Distinctive taxi livery (yellow body, checkered stripe)
- Taxi medallion or license plate markings
- Cars with illuminated roof signs (even at night)

## Common false positives
- Regular cars that are yellow but not taxis
- Rideshare vehicles (Uber/Lyft) without taxi markings
- Yellow or white vans, SUVs, or trucks
- Reflections of yellow objects on car surfaces
- Yellow signs, barriers, or construction equipment

## Edge cases
- Taxi partially visible at tile edge (roof sign or door visible)
- Taxi viewed from behind (look for taxi plate, roof sign)
- Night-time taxis identified by illuminated roof sign
- Taxis in traffic with only partial view
- Different colored taxis (green, white, black) — look for roof sign and markings
