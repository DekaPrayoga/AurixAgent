# Buses

## What to look for
- **Large rectangular body** — tall, boxy vehicle much bigger than cars
- **Multiple windows** in a row — passenger windows along the side
- **Front windshield** — large, often split or curved
- **Wheels** — usually 2 visible, larger than car wheels
- **Destination sign** — LED or placard on front/top showing route
- **Doors** — folding or sliding passenger doors on the side
- **Bright colors** — often yellow (school bus), or transit livery

## Common false positives (DO NOT select)
- Large vans or trucks (fewer windows, different proportions)
- RVs / motorhomes
- Box trucks
- Trams or trains

## Edge cases
- Bus at angle — look for the distinctive tall rectangular shape + row of windows
- Double-decker bus — very tall, windows on two levels
- Bus partially cut off — still count if body shape + windows visible
- School bus (yellow) — easy to identify by color
