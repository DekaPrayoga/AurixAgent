# Video Analysis

Analyze video content, extract claims, and verify information from video sources.

## Tools

- `youtube_transcript` - Extract transcripts from YouTube videos
- `video_agent` - Research pipeline agent for video content analysis

## Instructions

- Extract key claims and statements made in the video for verification
- Check credentials and expertise of speakers and cited sources
- Note missing context, omitted information, or one-sided presentations
- Cross-reference video claims with external sources and fact-checking sites
