# Deep Research

Conduct comprehensive research using the 13-agent research pipeline for thorough analysis.

## Tools

- `research` - ResearchPipeline with 13 specialized agents for deep investigation
- `web_search` - Search the web for information and sources
- `web_fetch` - Fetch and extract content from web pages

## Instructions

- Use /depth command to control research thoroughness (shallow, moderate, deep)
- Always cite sources with URLs and publication dates for credibility
- Separate factual information from opinions and speculation clearly
