# Citation Verification

Verify citations and ensure all referenced sources exist and are accurately represented.

## Tools

- `CitationGuardian` - Research pipeline agent for citation verification
- `web_search` - Search for and validate sources

## Instructions

- Verify every cited source exists and is accessible before finalizing research
- Check that quoted or referenced information is accurately represented
- Flag any potential fabrications, misquotes, or misrepresented data immediately
- Ensure citations include complete metadata (author, date, publisher, URL)
