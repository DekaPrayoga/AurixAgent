# GIF Search & Sharing

Find and share relevant GIFs that match the desired emotion, context, or tone.

## Tools

- `gif_search` - Search for and retrieve GIFs from online libraries

## Instructions

- Match the GIF to the emotional tone and conversational context of the request
- Keep selections appropriate for the audience and setting, avoiding offensive or ambiguous content
- Prefer widely recognizable GIFs from popular media to maximize relatability
- Offer multiple options when the ideal tone is unclear so the user can pick the best fit
