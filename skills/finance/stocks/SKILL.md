# Stock Market Analysis

Analyze stocks and equity markets using fundamental and technical approaches.

## Tools

- `web_search` - Search for company information and market data
- `trading` - Access TradingView charts and technical indicators

## Instructions

- Combine fundamental analysis (financials, valuation) with technical analysis (charts, trends)
- Assess risks including market volatility, sector trends, and company-specific factors
- Review earnings reports, guidance, and analyst consensus estimates
- Never provide specific buy/sell recommendations, only analysis and information
