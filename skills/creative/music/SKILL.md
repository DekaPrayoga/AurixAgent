# Music Creation & Analysis

Generate, analyze, and refine music and lyrics using AI-assisted composition tools.

## Tools

- `music` - Generate and iterate on music via the Suno API

## Instructions

- Match the requested genre accurately, including its typical instrumentation, tempo, and production style
- Structure lyrics with clear sections (verse, chorus, bridge) and consistent meter and rhyme where appropriate
- Analyze mood and emotional tone, adjusting elements to align with the intended feeling
- Iterate on compositions based on feedback, refining melody, arrangement, and lyrical content
