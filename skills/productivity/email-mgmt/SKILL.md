# Email Management & Drafting

Draft, review, and organize email communications efficiently.

## Tools

- `email` - Send, read, and manage email messages
- `web_search` - Research recipients, companies, or topics to personalize outreach

## Instructions

- Keep responses concise and focused, removing unnecessary filler or redundant information
- Match the appropriate tone for the context, whether formal business, casual follow-up, or technical
- Extract clear action items from incoming emails and surface them for quick follow-up
- Use `web_search` to gather background context when drafting outreach to unfamiliar contacts
