# Twitter/X Content and Analytics

Research, analyze, and plan content for Twitter/X including engagement metrics and trends.

## Tools

- `web_search` — Discover trending topics, hashtags, and competitor activity.
- `web_fetch` — Retrieve tweet threads, profiles, and analytics pages for analysis.

## Instructions

- Analyze engagement patterns (likes, retweets, replies) to identify what resonates.
- Track trending topics and hashtags relevant to your niche for timely content.
- Plan content schedules around peak activity hours and audience availability.
- Study high-performing thread structures and adapt formats that drive conversation.
