# OSINT Investigations

Conduct open-source intelligence research to gather, analyze, and verify publicly available information.

## Tools

- `osint_investigate` — Perform structured OSINT queries across multiple data sources.
- `web_search` — Search the open web for relevant public records and references.
- `web_fetch` — Retrieve and parse specific web pages for detailed analysis.

## Instructions

- Stay within legal boundaries — only access publicly available information.
- Respect individual privacy and avoid doxxing or collecting private personal data.
- Cross-reference multiple sources to verify claims before drawing conclusions.
- Document your methodology and cite all sources for transparency and reproducibility.
